Anikki is a free anime list for Android. There is no account, no server of ours and no analytics.

2. What is stored on your device

Everything you enter in Anikki stays on your phone:

  • your list (titles, status, rating, progress, notes, dates you set yourself);
  • your settings (language, switches, notifications) and, if you enter it, your name for the greeting;
  • the catalogue (the title data shipped with the app);
  • the schedule of notifications for new episodes (created on the device; there is no push server).

If you import a MyAnimeList export file, it is read on your phone and not uploaded. For this, Android places a copy of the file in the app's cache. It stays on your phone; Android may clear the cache, and uninstalling Anikki removes it.

With “Back up data” (Profile > Settings) Anikki writes a backup file to a place you choose yourself. It only leaves your phone if you share it or save it to a cloud folder yourself.

If Android device backup is turned on, Android may store Anikki’s data in your Google account backup. Android and Google control this, not Anikki; you can turn it off in the Android settings.

If you uninstall Anikki, this data is removed from your phone.

3. What Anikki loads from the internet and where data goes

Anikki does not send your list, ratings or notes to us or to any third party.

Anikki connects to these services:

DestinationWhenWhat is transferred
GitHub Pages (jvirtual1.github.io)at most once a dayTwo small JSON files: the airing schedule for new episodes and a control file for images (each with a checksum). GitHub sees your IP address.
Kitsu (media.kitsu.app)when showing coversAn image request, including your IP address.
Anime News Network (cdn.animenewsnetwork.com)when showing coversAn image request, including your IP address.
TVmaze (static.tvmaze.com)when showing coversAn image request, including your IP address.
Studio Ghibli (www.ghibli.jp)when showing coversAn image request, including your IP address.

These providers are separate controllers under their own privacy policies: GitHub, Anime News Network, TVmaze, Kitsu (provider's website), Studio Ghibli (provider's website). Some of them also process data outside the EU, for example in the USA; see their policies for details.

You can turn off image requests with the switch "Load covers from the web" (Profile > Settings). Then Anikki loads no images from these services and shows titles as designed covers.

The daily download of the airing schedule and the image control file from GitHub Pages does not depend on this switch. It happens at most once a day and, for the airing schedule, only when the airing times the app already has are older than three days or have expired.

If you install Anikki from Google Play, Google's own terms apply to that.

4. What Anikki does not do

  • no advertising;
  • no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs;
  • no account and no sign-in;
  • no upload of your list, ratings or MyAnimeList file;
  • no push notifications from a server (all notifications are created on the device).

If a later version changes this (for example advertising or paid features), this policy will be updated before the change and the app will say so.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21). Because the controller keeps no accounts or user data on its own servers, these rights mainly concern what is on your phone; you can delete it at any time in the app or by uninstalling.
