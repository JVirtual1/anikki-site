Data sources, licences and credits – Anikki

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Anikki is an independent, free app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of the services or companies named below. Names, trademarks and images belong to their respective owners. This page lists the sources of the data and images used in the app and their licences.

1. Catalogue data

manami-project / anime-offline-database

  • Licence: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Source: github.com/manami-project/anime-offline-database
  • Used: format, episode count, season, status, year, alternative titles, cover URLs and genres as the basis of the Anikki catalogue.
  • The Anikki catalogue is a derivative database and is offered under the ODbL 1.0. The catalogue file will be made available for download on this page together with the first app version published on the Play Store.
  • Attribution: "Contains data from manami-project / anime-offline-database, licensed under ODbL 1.0."

AniDB

  • Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike (CC BY-NC-SA). Source: anidb.net
  • Used: relations between titles (sequels, prequels, side stories).
  • Attribution: "Relations: AniDB (CC BY-NC-SA)."

Further sources

  • Wikidata (CC0 1.0): titles, streaming links and identifiers.
  • Kitsu: titles, streaming links and relations between titles.
  • TVmaze (CC BY-SA 4.0): dates and genres.
  • Anime News Network and Media Arts Database: individual title details and identifiers.

2. Air times

TVmaze

  • Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0). Source: tvmaze.com, tvmaze.com/api
  • Used: air times of episodes, adapted by Anikki for the airing schedule file that the app fetches from GitHub Pages. This adapted file is offered under CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Attribution: "Air times: TVmaze (CC BY-SA 4.0), adapted."
  • Cover images are also loaded from static.tvmaze.com (see below).

3. Cover images (linked, not self-hosted)

The app contains no cover images, and we do not host any. The app loads an image directly from the respective source when a cover is shown and keeps it only in the cache on the device; loading can be switched off in the app ("Load covers from the web"). The rights to the images belong to their respective owners.

Rights holders can report an image by e-mail to kontakt@anikki.app. We review every report promptly and block the image in the app.

Image sources currently in use:

  • Kitsu (media.kitsu.app)
  • Anime News Network (cdn.animenewsnetwork.com)
  • TVmaze (static.tvmaze.com)
  • Studio Ghibli (www.ghibli.jp)

4. Identifiers

MyAnimeList

  • Anikki uses MyAnimeList ids only as keys to match titles. Anikki does not fetch any data from MyAnimeList and shows no images or ratings from there. Some title values come from the openly licensed databases named above, which may in turn rest on MyAnimeList entries. A MyAnimeList export file is read only if you import it yourself, on the device, without upload.
  • MyAnimeList is a trademark of its owner. Anikki is not affiliated.
